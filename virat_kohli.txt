Virat Kohli is an Indian international cricketer widely regarded as one of the greatest 
batsmen in the history of the sport. Born on November 5, 1988, in Delhi, India,
he rose to fame after captaining the victorious Indian Under-19 team at the 2008 World Cup.
Known for his aggressive playing style, exceptional chasing ability, 
and strict fitness discipline, he holds numerous records, 
including the most centuries in One Day Internationals (ODIs). 
As a former all-format captain of the Indian national cricket team, 
he led his side to historic victories, including a monumental Test series win in Australia. 
Beyond his immense statistics, his relentless work ethic and passion 
continue to inspire millions of fans and young athletes worldwide.
